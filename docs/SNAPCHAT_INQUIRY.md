## Zpráva pro DRESSX

Subject: Small studio asking about Bitmoji Fashion on Snapchat

```
Hi,

I run Ol1n Market (https://market.ol1n.com), a small store based in Czechia that sells
stylised 3D avatar accessories: helmets, backpacks, bags and similar pieces. The models
are low-poly, textured and delivered as FBX and GLB. They are made with our own
AI-assisted pipeline and reviewed by hand before listing. The store is new and the
catalogue is still small.

I read your article about the Bitmoji avatar store on Snapchat and would like to
understand whether there is a path for a studio of our size:

1. Is Bitmoji Fashion open only to brands with a direct agreement with Snap, or can
   a small studio or independent creator get items in, directly or through a partner
   such as DRESSX?
2. If it goes through you, what does a typical engagement look like for a small
   catalogue: minimum volume, costs, revenue share?
3. Does the store take accessories (headwear, backpacks), or only clothing?
4. Are the technical requirements (format, rig, polygon budget) documented anywhere
   we could read before taking more of your time?

A short answer or a pointer to the right person is enough. Thank you.

<name>
Ol1n Market
```

Hi,

I run Ol1n Market (https://market.ol1n.com), a small store based in Czechia that sells

Two short questions about Bitmoji Fashion:

1. Is there a programme, or a plan for one, that lets independent creators or small

Thank you.

<name>
Ol1n Market
